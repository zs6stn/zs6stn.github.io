Our EchoLink callsigns are ZS6STN-R (Node No:901594) and ZS6STN-L (Node No: 920558).

ZS6STN-R is connected to our VHF repeater and is running on a <a href="https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/raspberry-pi-5/" target="_blank">Raspberry Pi 5 4GB</a> with <a href="https://www.allstarlink.org/" target="_blank">AllStarLink Version 3</a>.

ZS6STN-L is hosted at Terreco running on a Linux VPS server which is the Master Node for [ZA-Net](https://www.za-net.org.za){:target="_blank"}.
