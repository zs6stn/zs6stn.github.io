### Licenced Since
- 1988

### How you started
- Started with CB radio in the early 1980's while in high school and was fascinated with DX communications globally on 11m. Decided to study electronics engineering instead of law - a wise choice motivated by Radio!  Wrote the RAE while studying via the Air Force to become ZR6AST.  Sadly never managed to get the Morse done - maybe one day!
Most Memorable Contacts

### Most Memorable Contact
- Been a few - a SSB QRP contact using 300mW and a (tr)USDX/ dipole, long path SSB DX's with New Mexico and Nevada, contacts with Interesting operators all over the world!  SOTA and POTA contacts from mountain tops and safari parks are always memorable. 

### Best part of the Hobby
- There is always something new to explore, new technology, new modes, different antennas, changing propagation, exploring space weather, optimising field operations. emergency comms, there is never a reason to be bored.  The cameraderie of the Ham community, interest groups like SOTA/ POTA/ HOTA  and Club activity is a terrific social aspect of the hobby too.


### Favorite Modes
- HF SSB DX, playing with digital modes like FT8/ JSCall/ VARAC, ragchewing on 2m occasionally

### About Mike 
- Been in technical roles in Air Force, medical informatics, nuclear medicine and then moved into research, management and consultancy to the IT and Telecoms sector globally for last 40 years. Lived and worked in North America, Middle East and Africa.
