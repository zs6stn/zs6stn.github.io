## SARC Year-End Function & Newbie QSO Party

**Saturday, November 15, 2025 – Keith’s QTH (ZS6HI)**

The Sandton Amateur Radio Club wrapped up the year in style with a combined **Year-End Function** and the eagerly anticipated **Newbie QSO Party**. Keith (ZS6HI) generously hosted the event at his beautiful QTH, which provided plenty of space for antennas, operating positions, a nice braai fire, and good conversation.

## Antennas in the Air

The day began with members setting up multiple antennas around the property:

- A **linked dipole** for multiband HF  
- An **End-Fed Half Wave (EFHW)** for flexible operating  

Operators wasted no time—within minutes, the bands were alive with SARC newcomers making contacts under the guidance of experienced club members.

![Busy newbies on the air](/assets/images/posts/2025-12-07-sarc-yef/busy-newbies.jpg)
*Newbie operators diving into their first QSOs with plenty of hands-on help*

## Hands-On Learning & Mentorship

New operators were encouraged to get on the air, test their confidence, and learn proper operating habits.  
Seasoned operators moved around the field, offering support, coaching, and guidance on:

- HF operating etiquette  
- Logging and QSO flow  
- Antenna behaviour  
- Band conditions and propagation  

![Guidance from seasoned operators](/assets/images/posts/2025-12-07-sarc-yef/guidance-from-seasoned-operators.jpg)
*Experienced members helping new operators settle into their first HF contacts*

## Fresh Air & Field Radio Fun

Before the weather turned, the operating environment was perfect:  
wide lawns, shade, fresh air, and plenty of space for antennas.  
Members chatted, operated, experimented, and enjoyed the relaxed atmosphere.

![Out in the fresh air](/assets/images/posts/2025-12-07-sarc-yef/out-in-the-fresh-air.jpg)

![More outdoor operating fun](/assets/images/posts/2025-12-07-sarc-yef/out-in-the-fresh-air-2.jpg)

## Rain Arrives — The Braai Begins

Eventually the rain rolled in, forcing the group indoors.  
But moving inside didn't slow the spirit of the event—in fact, it kicked off the second half of the fun: **braai time**.

![Year-end braai indoors](/assets/images/posts/2025-12-07-sarc-yef/yef-braai.jpg)
*Good food, good company, and plenty of radio talk indoors while the rain fell outside*

## Awards, Socialising & ISS Attempt

Between braai smoke and lively conversations, the club held its **informal awards and acknowledgements**.  
A lot of laughs were shared and several standout members received recognition for their contributions during 2025.

Later in the afternoon, members attempted to work the **International Space Station (ISS)** on a VHF pass — but the radio gods weren’t cooperating, and little success was achieved. Still, watching members huddled under a gazebo from the rain, trying to track the ISS with a handheld Yagi was a quite a highlight of the day.

![Trying to pick up the ISS](/assets/images/posts/2025-12-07-sarc-yef/trying-to-pick-up-the-iss.jpg)
*Operators attempting an ISS contact using a handheld Yagi and live tracking*

## A Fantastic End to 2025

Despite the rain, the event was a massive success.  
The combination of newbie excitement, technical experimentation, and relaxed social time made it a perfect closing chapter for the club’s year.

**A heartfelt thank you to Keith (ZS6HI) for hosting, and to everyone who contributed equipment, experience, and food.**

Here’s to even more curiosity, learning, and radio adventures in 2026!

**73 from SARC — Where Curiosity Finds a Frequency**
