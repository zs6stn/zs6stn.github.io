To all our new operators: you've taken a significant step into a community that spans continents, builds friendships, and communicates beyond boundaries. Remember to keep your antenna high and your spirit higher; every "CQ" you send is an opportunity to learn and connect.

Don't hesitate to ask questions; there's always someone willing to help. Experiment, enjoy, and above all, keep safety and regulation at the forefront.

We look forward to hearing your signals on the bands and seeing you participate in club activities, field days, nets, and beyond. Welcome aboard!

73 from everyone at ZS6STN.
