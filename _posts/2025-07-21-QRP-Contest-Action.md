## SARL QRP Contest – Winter Edition

**Saturday, July 19, 2025 – Parks and Pavements Across Gauteng**

The Sandton Amateur Radio Club turned out in full force this past Saturday morning to take part in the **SARL QRP Contest – Winter Leg**. Running from **07:00 to 09:00 UTC**, the contest tested both operator skill and station efficiency—all while embracing the spirit of low-power HF operation.

## Energy Building the Night Before

The excitement was already buzzing the evening before. The club's WhatsApp group lit up with members announcing their planned locations, antenna setups, and tactics. From nature reserves to backyards, members were plotting their strategy, checking propagation forecasts, and prepping their gear.

By Saturday morning, it seemed **every nearby park had a ZS6STN operator** in it—dipoles stretching between trees, masts rising from picnic benches, and radios humming with potential. The camaraderie and energy were palpable.

## Band-by-Band Breakdown

The contest format was split into two distinct one-hour segments:

### First Hour: 40m Band
From 07:00 to 08:00 UTC, **everyone was on 40 meters**, and local propagation didn't disappoint. Contacts flew back and forth between club members scattered across Gauteng. Signals were strong, and the exchange of grid squares and QRP power levels was efficient and fun.

### Second Hour: 20m, 40m, or 10m – Your Choice
At 08:00 UTC, things opened up. Operators had the option to jump to **20m or 10m** to hunt for those all-important **long-distance (DX) contacts**, or remain on 40m to dig deeper into local QSOs. Some club members reported good skip conditions and even snagged a few DX points to boost their logs.
