## Second HF Assessment Session: 100% Pass Rate!

**📅 Saturday, September 27, 2025**
**📍 Justin (ZS6JGP)'s QTH**

The second HF assessment day for the October 2025 RAE students was held this past Saturday—and once again, every single participant achieved a pass! The students who couldn't attend the first session showed true dedication and persistence, and their hard work was rewarded with a flawless 100% success rate.

## Setting the Scene

The day kicked off early at Justin's (ZS6JGP) QTH, where he welcomed the students—many of whom were meeting face-to-face for the first time after weeks of online training. The atmosphere was alive with a mix of nerves, excitement, and anticipation. Even the weather seemed to cooperate, setting the stage for a memorable session.

![Students gathered around the assessment station](/assets/images/second-hf-assessment-table-setup.jpeg)  
*Students reviewing procedures and preparing for their individual assessments at the outdoor station*

Brandon (ZS6LZ) and Justin (ZS6JGP) guided the group through a slightly different setup compared to the first assessment. This time, two stations on different bands were made available (courtesy of Brandon's eagerness to expand and go above and beyond for the students), providing more variety and real-world exposure for the students.

Together, the assessors demonstrated proper station setup, emphasizing both correct procedures and critical safety aspects—such as configuring an inverted-V dipole antenna. Their efforts paid off: students not only made local contacts but also logged several exciting DX connections.

![Inverted-V dipole antenna setup](/assets/images/second-hf-assessment-antenna.jpeg)  
*The inverted-V dipole antenna configured for multi-band operation at Justin's QTH*

## On the Air

Soon enough, it was time for each student to step up to the microphone. One by one, all five participants confidently called CQ, established their required contacts, and proved their readiness under the watchful guidance of Brandon and Justin.

![Student operating with assessors observing](/assets/images/second-hf-assessment-operating.jpeg)  
*A student making their required contacts while assessors provide guidance and support*

It was inspiring to watch nerves melt away as students transitioned into confident operators. Each successful QSO added momentum, boosting the confidence of those still waiting for their turn.

## Results: Five Students, Five Passes 🎉

While some students completed their written test components, others worked through their practical on-air assessments. By the end of the day, all five students had passed their HF assessments—a remarkable outcome that reflects both their commitment and the high standard of instruction they've received.

![Group photo of successful students with assessors](/assets/images/second-hf-assessment-group-photo.jpeg)  
*Celebrating success: all five students with their assessors after achieving a perfect pass rate*

With the HF practical portion successfully completed, the students are now well-prepared for the RAE exam in October 2025.

## Thanks & Congratulations

A huge word of thanks goes to Brandon (ZS6LZ) and Justin (ZS6JGP) for their professionalism, patience, and encouragement throughout the day. Their guidance created the perfect environment for student success, and their dedication to training the next generation of amateur radio operators deserves special recognition.

👏 **Congratulations once again to all five students on your achievement! We wish you the very best of luck for your upcoming RAE exam, and 73 from the assessment team!**
