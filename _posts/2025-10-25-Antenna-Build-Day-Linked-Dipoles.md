## From Exam Room to Build Table

Following the successful RAE examination on 18 October 2025, our newest operators joined a special Antenna Build Day to turn fresh theory into confident practice. Under the guidance of seasoned club mentors, each participant constructed a  linked dipole from start to finish.

![Mentors and new operators setting up the build area](/assets/images/antenna-build-3.jpeg)  
*Kick-off briefing before tools and tape measures come out.*

## Measuring, Cutting, and Tuning

The hands-on session focused on accurate measuring, clean cutting, careful link assembly, and deliberate tuning. Using antenna analysers, builders trimmed each section until every link was resonant across its intended band segment—an invaluable lesson in how small adjustments change real-world performance.

![Participants trimming link wires at the workbench](/assets/images/antenna-build-2.jpeg)  
*Hands-on learning while assembling the dipole links.*

## Mentorship in Action

This new initiative gave recent RAE students direct access to experienced operators who shared build techniques, troubleshooting tips, and on-air stories. The atmosphere mixed focus and fun, with plenty of laughter (and the occasional dropped pliers) keeping spirits high throughout the workshop.

## Braai, Radios, and New Rigs

After crimping the links, everyone gathered for a relaxed bring-and-braai. Matthew (ZS6MDV) brought along a selection of radios for the new Hams to explore, fielding questions about station setups, rig choices, and HF operating habits. The informal mentorship helped every participant map out their next steps on the air.

## Ready for the Bands

We cannot wait to hear these new operators calling CQ with their own home-built linked dipoles. From everyone at ZS6STN—welcome aboard, and 73!
