## Evening at the Clubhouse
**Wednesday, June 25, 2025 - ZS6STN Clubhouse, Bryanston**

What started as a planned antenna demonstration turned into one of those memorable club evenings that stretched well beyond expectations. Our doors opened at 17:30 as promised, but the engaging conversations and hands-on learning kept us going until 08:30!

## Pico Balloons and High-Altitude Adventures

The evening kicked off with an exciting discussion about the two ZS6SRC pico balloons currently aloft. Members gathered around tracking displays, analysing flight paths and examining telemetry data. The fascination with these tiny, globe-trotting beacons sparked animated conversations about propagation, power efficiency, and the remarkable distances these lightweight packages can travel.

## Hands-On Learning: EFHW Antenna Dissection

![EFHW Antenna Components](/assets/images/2025-06-26-efhw-components.jpeg)
*EFHW antenna components laid out for examination - balun, wire, and connectors ready for hands-on learning*

The highlight of the evening was our informal but thorough examination of an End-Fed Half-Wave (EFHW) antenna system. Rather than a formal presentation, this became an interactive exploration as members took turns examining the components and asking questions.

The 49:1 balun construction became a particular focus of discussion, with experienced builders sharing insights about:

- Wire diameter considerations and their impact on performance
- Construction techniques for reliable 49:1 transformers
- Comparisons with 1:1 baluns and their different applications
- The role of EF 9:1 antennas in the antenna toolkit

## Antenna Comparisons and Technical Insights

The technical discussions expanded to cover a wide range of antenna topics. Members compared the characteristics of:

- Linked dipoles and their versatility
- Vertical antennas and their radiation patterns
- Magnetic loop antennas and their unique properties
- Bandwidth considerations across different antenna types

These comparisons provided valuable insights for both new and experienced operators, highlighting the trade-offs and applications where each antenna type excels.

## Club Community and Camaraderie

![Club Members in Discussion](/assets/images/2025-06-26-club-discussion.jpeg)
*Members engaged in animated technical discussions at the clubhouse*

Throughout the evening, the social aspect of our club shone through. Hot coffee kept the conversations flowing, though the rusks remained largely untouched – a testament to how engaging the discussions were!

We had our new club business cards available for members, along with badges for our recently licensed operators. For those who haven't collected their badges yet, we have them ready and waiting at future meetings.

## Looking Forward

Evenings like this remind us why amateur radio clubs are so valuable. The combination of technical learning, practical experience sharing, and genuine camaraderie creates an environment where knowledge flows freely and friendships deepen.

Whether you're interested in high-altitude ballooning, antenna theory, or simply enjoy good technical conversations with fellow enthusiasts, our Wednesday evening gatherings offer something for everyone.

## Join Us Next Time

If you missed this session, don't worry – the club meets every last Wednesday of the month at our clubhouse at 11 Kildoon Road, Bryanston, from 17:30 to 20:30 (though as this meeting proved, we sometimes extend the hours when the conversation is particularly engaging!). Coffee and cookies are always available, and you never know what fascinating topics will emerge.

Thanks to everyone who participated and made this such a worthwhile and educational evening. The enthusiasm and knowledge sharing that characterised this meeting perfectly embodies the spirit of amateur radio.
