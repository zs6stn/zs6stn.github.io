## HF Assessment Success: All Five Students Pass

**Friday, August 16, 2025 – Keith ZS6Hi's QTH**

Another successful milestone reached for our RAE October 2025 students! Five dedicated students gathered at Keith ZS6Hi's QTH for their **HF Assessment**, a crucial practical component of their journey toward obtaining their amateur radio licence.

## Setting the Stage

The assessment day began with proper introductions as the students met face-to-face, many for the first time since their online training sessions. The atmosphere was a perfect blend of nerves and excitement as everyone prepared for this important practical evaluation.

**Keith ZS6Hi** and **Mark ZS6MDX** led the group through the **HF Assessment Preparation Guide**, ensuring each student understood exactly what would be expected during their individual assessments. This thorough briefing helped calm pre-assessment jitters and reinforced the key operating procedures they'd been learning.

![Students receiving assessment briefing on the covered patio](/assets/images/hf-assessment-briefing.jpg)  
*Keith ZS6Hi leading the group through the HF Assessment Preparation Guide while students listen attentively*

## Hands-On Field Station Setup

Theory quickly turned to practice as the group moved outdoors to set up a proper field station. Under expert guidance, the students learned to:

- **Erect a linked dipole antenna** for multi-band operation
- **Configure the Xiegu G90** for field conditions
- **Optimize antenna tuning for best performance**

This hands-on experience proved invaluable, showing students how amateur radio works beyond the classroom and demonstrating the portable nature of our hobby.

![Student operating the radio with instructors watching](/assets/images/hf-assessment-student-operating.jpg)

## On-Air Success

The real test came when each student took their turn at the microphone. **All five students successfully called CQ** and managed to make their required contacts—a testament to their preparation and the quality instruction they'd received throughout the course.

Watching nervous students transform into confident operators as they completed their contacts was truly rewarding. The excitement was infectious as each successful QSO built confidence for the next student's turn.

## Assessment Results: 100% Pass Rate

While some students worked through their individual practical assessments, others completed a written test component. The day concluded with **all five students successfully passing their HF Assessment**—an outstanding achievement that speaks volumes about their dedication and the quality of instruction provided.

## Looking Forward

With this HF Assessment complete, our students are well-prepared for the **RAE exam in October 2025**. For those who missed this opportunity, mark your calendars: **our final club HF Assessment is scheduled for September 27th**.
