## RAE Online Practical: First Steps On-Air

**Thursday, July 17, 2025 – Microsoft Teams**

Last Thursday marked an exciting milestone for the RAE October 2025 student group as they participated in their very first **online practical session** focused on amateur radio **operating procedures**. Held via Microsoft Teams, the session offered students the chance to step into the role of a radio operator and practice proper voice procedures in a safe, supportive environment.

## A Strong Turnout

With **13 students** attending, the session exceeded expectations and required a bit of quick thinking and teamwork. The group was split into **two smaller teams** to ensure everyone had a chance to actively participate and receive individual attention.

To accommodate the larger turnout, we were fortunate to have **four dedicated lecturers** step in to guide each group:

## Learning by Doing

Each student created a **mock callsign** for the evening and took turns participating in simulated QSO scenarios. This hands-on approach gave everyone a chance to **practice real-world etiquette**, including calling procedures, exchanging signal reports, and closing contacts—all critical skills for new hams preparing for their licence.

The energy was infectious, and the students had a **blast learning the ropes**. Even through the screen, the excitement was clear: voices grew more confident, terminology became more natural, and camaraderie began to build among this new wave of amateur operators.

## Looking Ahead

This online practical is the first of several training opportunities leading up to the **October 2025 RAE exam**, and it set a strong tone for what's to come. With enthusiastic students and committed mentors, the future of amateur radio in our community looks bright.

To the students—keep practicing! And to the lecturers—thank you for being the bridge to the next generation of hams.

**73 and good DX to all our future operators!**
