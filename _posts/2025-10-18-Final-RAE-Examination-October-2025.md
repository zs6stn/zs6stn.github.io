## A Milestone Saturday at SARL HQ

On Saturday, 18 October 2025, the final RAE (Radio Amateur Examination) session for the year was held at the SARL headquarters, located at Sentech, Octave Street, Radiokop. A total of 15 students took their seats, marking the culmination of months of hard work, late-night study sessions, and endless revision.

![Students writing the final RAE at SARL headquarters](/assets/images/finalexam.jpeg)  
*Focused faces during the October 2025 examination session.*

## Dedication on Display

The air was thick with anticipation and a fair bit of nervous energy as each candidate prepared to demonstrate the knowledge and skills they had gained through dedication and perseverance. From mastering the complex world of radio theory and electronics, to understanding propagation, regulations, and safety, every student invested countless hours into reaching this milestone.

The camaraderie built among classmates and instructors alike created an environment of support and shared excitement for what lay ahead.

## Stepping Into the Amateur Community

As the exams concluded, there was a collective sigh of relief and an unspoken pride in knowing that each participant had taken one step closer to joining the proud community of South African Radio Amateurs.

To all who wrote: well done! You have not only tested your knowledge but proven your commitment to the spirit of Amateur Radio - learning, sharing, and advancing the art of communication. From everyone at ZS6STN, a great big 73!
